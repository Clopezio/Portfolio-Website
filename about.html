About me

Umut Hastan

Software Developer

I'm Umut, a computer science student at ITIS Magistri Cumacini. I got into programming because I kept wanting to know what was happening behind the screen.

These days I'm building stronger foundations in Java, C++ and Python, experimenting with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and learning more about AI and software architecture. I learn best by making something, finding out where it breaks, and trying again with a better understanding.

Alongside school, I'm working on Xenyor and contributing to Piero Cassano's website. Both are giving me the chance to turn what I study into practical experience.

Italian and Turkish are my native languages, and I speak English at C1 level. I'm still early in my career, so I don't pretend to have every answer. I ask questions, take feedback seriously, and care about leaving the work better than I found it.
